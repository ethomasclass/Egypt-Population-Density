# You Lead Egypt — the slideshow

`you-lead-egypt.html` — one standalone file. Open it in any browser. No install, no build, no
network needed (web fonts fall back to system serif/sans if offline).

35 minutes, middle school, pairs at one device. Students play four real Egyptian presidents and
make four real decisions, learning all three density types through Egypt's actual history.

## How it runs

19 slides, driven by the bottom nav (or arrow keys). On the four **decision** slides the Next
button stays disabled until the pair picks an option — that gate is what forces the conversation.
Each consequence slide echoes their choice back before revealing what the real president did.

A teal **density dashboard** appears on the consequence slides showing Egypt's three numbers at
that point in history, so all three stay visible rather than being mentioned once.

## Companion pieces

## Design notes

1280×720, scaled to fit any screen. Palette and type are documented in the spec. One deliberate
call: **no ancient-Egypt visual clichés** — this is a lab about people alive today.
